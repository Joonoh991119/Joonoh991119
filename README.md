### Research Interests

**Human behavior**
- **Magnitude estimation** across dimensions — size, pitch, time, numerosity, and value
- **Context-dependent normalization**, **history effects**, and the **granularity effect**

**Computational models**
- *Cognitive models* — Bayesian observer models of prior knowledge and uncertainty; information-theoretic models of the brain's limited resources
- *Neural models* — task-optimized RNNs for temporal dynamics (e.g., accumulation and drift in working memory) and task-relevant neural alignment; generative models (e.g., β-VAE) as a basis for resource-optimal Bayesian models; CNNs for simulating the emergence of brain-like representations
